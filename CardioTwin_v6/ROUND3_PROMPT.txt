AFE link (important, differs from a generic SPI or I2C design)
- The AFE4950EVM talks to a host over USB CDC serial. It does NOT use SPI or I2C to the AM62B. Do not design SPI, I2C or device-tree work for it.
- Evidence: at boot the board saw the AFE as a full-speed USB device (VID 2047, PID 0300) with two cdc_acm interfaces (ttyACM0 and ttyACM1). About 12.9 seconds after boot the device disconnected and never came back. The cause is unknown. The replug test was never completed.
- TI capture protocol, taken from TI's own BASE_PROFILE1 scripts (searchPort.py, regCapture.py): send "C:CDC?" and the device answers CAPTURE or REG_EDIT. Baud 128000. Start with "C:CAP_START" plus two hex digits plus eight hex digits. Stop with "C:CAP_STOP". Data arrives as tab-separated hex text lines of 24-bit signed words. Volts = code x 1.2 / 2^21. The word order is in the *_FIFO_SEQ.cfg file. The registers must be programmed first by the TI GUI (import AFE4950_Settings.cfg). The 11-word cycle holds 10 ECG words and 1 PPG word. ECG is 500 Hz, PPG is 50 Hz.
